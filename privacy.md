# RackKeeper Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 7, 2026

RackKeeper is an offline app for managing server room racks, equipment, connections, floor maps and asset registers on your device. The developer does not collect or receive any of your information.

## Information we collect

None. The app has no server, no accounts, and no analytics, advertising or tracking. It does not send your data over the internet.

## Information the app stores on your device

- **Data you enter:** rack and equipment names, asset numbers, IP addresses, contact names and other records you type in or import.
- **Device key and license:** a signing key that identifies this device, and the license issued by your administrator.

This information is stored only in encrypted storage on your device. The signing key and storage key are kept in the iOS Keychain, bound to this device and excluded from iCloud backup.

## When information leaves your device

Only when you send it yourself:

- When you send a sync file (`.rksync`) or exchange file (`.rkdata`) by AirDrop, email or another app. These files are encrypted with a password you choose.
- When you send a license request file (`.rkrequest`) to your administrator. It contains only the device ID and public key.
- When you export to Excel or CSV. These files are not encrypted.

You choose the app or person to send to in the iOS share sheet.

## Retention and deletion

Your data stays on your device until you delete it or delete the app. Deleting the app removes its data from the device.
